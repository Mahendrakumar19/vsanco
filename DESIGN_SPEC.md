# Page-by-Page Design Specification

## Creative direction
Premium enterprise technology: calm, precise, modern, credible. Use Brixbyte as a reference for a clear partner-led solution narrative and conversion structure, not as a source to copy. Visual identity must remain VMC Media's own.

### Global layout
- Max content width: approximately 1200–1280px.
- Desktop grid: 12 columns; generous horizontal gutters.
- Page rhythm: 80–112px section spacing on desktop; 48–72px on mobile.
- Corner radii: 12–20px; avoid over-rounded toy-like cards.
- Visuals: authentic network, communication, cloud, and business-workflow imagery; product diagrams where more useful than stock photography.
- Motion: subtle entrance and hover transitions; respect reduced-motion settings.
- Every page should provide a clear primary CTA without overwhelming the user.

## 1. Home `/`
1. **Header:** VMC Media logo; Solutions mega-menu; Industries; Resources; About; Contact; Request a Quote.
2. **Hero:** headline such as “Business connectivity and communication, built around your needs.” Supporting copy that states VMC Media helps businesses explore approved TTBS solutions. Primary CTA: “Talk to a Solutions Expert”; secondary: “Explore Solutions”. Visual: abstract network topology / connected office nodes.
3. **Trust / partnership strip:** approved partner wording and logo only after authorization confirmation. Never suggest the site is the official Tata website.
4. **Business challenges:** unreliable connectivity, distributed teams, missed calls, disconnected communications, security requirements. Map each challenge to an approved solution.
5. **Solutions grid:** 4–6 top-level categories with concise descriptions and links.
6. **Featured solution spotlight:** choose a currently authorized offering (e.g., Smartflo only if approved); explain use case and CTA.
7. **How engagement works:** understand requirement → recommend suitable option → coordinate next steps. Do not promise service activation timelines.
8. **Industries:** selected industry tiles only when the mapped products are supported.
9. **FAQ preview:** 4–6 real questions.
10. **Closing CTA:** request a callback / quotation.
11. **Footer:** verified contact details, legal pages, partner disclaimer, official TTBS link.

## 2. About `/about`
- Hero: “A practical partner for business connectivity and communications.”
- Company introduction and service approach.
- Partnership explanation using exact approved designation.
- “How we help” steps: understand requirement, identify suitable offerings, coordinate enquiry and follow-up.
- Team/company facts only if verified.
- CTA to discuss a requirement.
- Avoid unsubstantiated “leading”, “number one”, customer counts, awards or certifications.

## 3. Solutions overview `/solutions`
- Intro that frames the catalogue by business outcome.
- Category cards grouped into Connectivity, Communications, Messaging, Collaboration, Cloud, Security and Managed Services where approved.
- Filter by business need only if there is enough content to make it useful.
- CTA after category groups.
- No pricing claims unless approved and maintained.

## 4. Connectivity `/solutions/connectivity`
- Hero focused on connecting offices, employees and business applications.
- Explain relevant authorized services, such as business internet / leased line / network services only after availability confirmation.
- Use-case blocks: office connectivity, multi-location network, bandwidth-sensitive operations.
- Selection considerations: location, bandwidth, redundancy, static IP/security needs, number of sites.
- FAQ and quote form.
- Do not promise availability at a specific address before a provider serviceability check.

## 5. Business communications `/solutions/business-communications`
- Explain cloud-based voice and business communication categories.
- Approved Smartflo and other communication products may be included only after catalogue approval.
- Feature blocks should describe outcomes (routing, agent workflows, call visibility, contact-centre operations) only where supported by the selected product.
- Use a simple architecture diagram: customer → communication platform → agent/team → business system, only if technically accurate.
- CTA: discuss communication requirements.

## 6. Business messaging `/solutions/business-messaging`
- Separate approved messaging channels such as SMS, WhatsApp Business Platform, RCS, OBD or email platform where authorized.
- Explain use cases: notifications, service updates, customer engagement, campaign messaging.
- Clearly distinguish platform capabilities from outcomes that depend on customer consent, templates, policies and third-party approvals.
- Do not claim guaranteed blue ticks, delivery, reach, read rates or conversions.
- CTA: request a messaging consultation.

## 7. Collaboration & productivity `/solutions/collaboration-productivity`
- Include Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Zoom or other named products only if authorized.
- Focus on collaboration, meetings, productivity and administration.
- Avoid implying that VMC Media is the software publisher.
- CTA: discuss collaboration requirements.

## 8. Cloud infrastructure `/solutions/cloud-infrastructure`
- Include approved cloud, migration, managed cloud or backup/archival services only when the catalogue is verified.
- Describe the service scope and responsibility split clearly.
- Avoid guarantees of recovery point, recovery time, data residency or compliance unless contractually documented.
- CTA: plan a cloud discussion.

## 9. Cybersecurity `/solutions/cybersecurity`
- Include only authorized security products/services.
- Explain scope, deployment prerequisites and customer responsibilities.
- No absolute claims such as “100% secure” or “prevents all attacks”.
- CTA: review business security needs.

## 10. Managed services `/solutions/managed-services`
- Explain which services are actually delivered by TTBS, VMC Media, or a third-party provider.
- Define onboarding and support boundaries clearly.
- Publish SLAs and support hours only from approved contract documents.
- CTA: discuss managed service needs.

## 11. Solution detail `/solutions/[slug]`
Reusable template:
- Breadcrumbs.
- Product/solution name and plain-language summary.
- Provider attribution and approved partner context.
- Who it is for / business challenges addressed.
- Core capabilities (verified only).
- Use cases and prerequisites.
- What to consider before requesting a quote.
- FAQs.
- Related approved solutions.
- Enquiry form preselected to this solution.
- Disclaimer and official provider link.
Avoid duplicating provider website copy. Write original summaries based on authorized product material.

## 12. Industries `/industries` and `/industries/[slug]`
- Industry cards for only the verticals that have meaningful approved use cases.
- Each detail page connects an industry problem to a specific authorized offering.
- Use sector-appropriate imagery and terminology.
- Do not imply regulatory compliance or certifications unless documented.
- Include relevant FAQs and preselected enquiry CTA.

## 13. Resources `/resources` and `/resources/[slug]`
- Search/filter by topic only when resource count justifies it.
- Article layout: title, author/reviewer, publish/update dates, reading time, table of contents, original body, references, related solutions, CTA.
- Product explainers must be reviewed against current provider documentation.
- Use original writing; do not republish protected brochures or articles without permission.

## 14. FAQs `/faqs`
- Group questions by connectivity, communications, messaging, cloud/security, quotations and support.
- Answers must not create unsupported service promises.
- Use accessible accordion patterns.
- Add FAQ structured data only when eligible under current search-engine guidance; do not expect guaranteed rich results.

## 15. Contact `/contact`
- Show verified business phone, email, hours and office address only after owner approval.
- Contact form, callback request and map only if address is public and confirmed.
- Explain what happens after submission.
- Keep partner contact routes distinct from TTBS official support.

## 16. Request a Quote `/request-a-quote`
- Short reassurance and a multi-step form only if it materially improves completion.
- Step 1: contact and business details.
- Step 2: solution interest and requirement.
- Step 3: consent and submit.
- Show progress, validation, errors, success and retry.
- Offer a simple single-page form if user testing shows multi-step friction.

## 17. Thank-you `/thank-you`
- Confirm that the request was received only after server-side success.
- State the next step without promising a specific response time unless the business has approved it.
- Provide links to relevant solution pages.
- Noindex this route.

## 18. Legal pages
- Privacy policy: data collected, purpose, sharing, retention, rights/contact, cookies/analytics, processors and grievance contact as legally reviewed.
- Terms: acceptable use, intellectual property, content accuracy, limitation language reviewed by counsel.
- Partner disclaimer: explain VMC Media's exact authorized relationship; distinguish it from TTBS; clarify that final product/service terms are governed by provider contracts.
