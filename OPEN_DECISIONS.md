# Open Decisions and Launch Blockers

These items require a business owner or authorized partner confirmation. Do not silently invent answers.

## P0 — must resolve before public launch
1. **Brand confirmation:** This project uses VMC Media based on prior context. Confirm this is the intended “another existing brand”.
2. **Domain:** Confirm the production domain and canonical hostname.
3. **Partner designation:** Provide the exact approved wording describing the relationship with Tata Tele Business Services / Tata Teleservices.
4. **Authorization evidence:** Confirm which products VMC Media may promote, sell, quote or support.
5. **Brand assets:** Obtain approved VMC logo and any permitted TTBS partner logo/lockup, with usage rules.
6. **Product catalogue:** Final list of products/services, accurate names, current descriptions and official URLs.
7. **Commercial policy:** Are public prices allowed? If not, use enquiry-based pricing.
8. **Lead handling:** Which email/CRM receives leads? Who owns assignment and follow-up?
9. **Contact details:** Confirm phone, email, office address, working hours and WhatsApp number, if applicable.
10. **Privacy:** Approve privacy notice, consent text, retention schedule and legal contact.
11. **Hosting:** Choose existing VPS/reverse proxy or managed hosting; confirm DNS access.
12. **Analytics:** Confirm analytics provider and cookie/consent requirements.

## P1 — resolve before full content rollout
- Industries to prioritize.
- Approved customer testimonials/case studies and permissions.
- Languages (English only initially or Hindi too).
- Resource content owner and review cadence.
- CRM integration method and duplicate-handling policy.
- Whether a CMS/admin panel is necessary at launch.
- Whether a solution-finder wizard adds value.
- Accessibility and performance acceptance thresholds.
