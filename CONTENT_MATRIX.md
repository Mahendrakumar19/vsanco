## Content model for each product page
1. Approved product name.
2. Provider attribution and approved partner wording.
3. Plain-language summary.
4. Ideal business use cases.
5. Supported capabilities (source-backed).
6. Prerequisites and dependencies.
7. Important limitations / availability notes.
8. FAQs.
9. Related approved solutions.
10. CTA and preselected enquiry.
11. Official provider reference link.
12. Internal source/approval record (not necessarily public).

## Content voice
- Professional, clear, direct and practical.
- Explain technical concepts in buyer language.
- Use “may help”, “designed to”, or precise capability language when outcomes depend on context.
- Avoid vague hype and unsupported superlatives.
- Never promise savings, uptime, delivery rates or conversions without substantiation.
