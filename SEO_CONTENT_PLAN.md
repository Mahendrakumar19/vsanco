# SEO and Content Plan

## 1. Search intent
Target high-intent business queries around connectivity, cloud telephony, enterprise messaging and collaboration. Product availability and exact service names must be verified before publication.

Potential topic clusters:
- Business internet and leased-line connectivity.
- Cloud telephony and business communication.
- Cloud contact centre / UCaaS / CPaaS (only if approved).
- Business SMS, WhatsApp Business Platform and RCS (only if approved).
- Collaboration and productivity suites.
- Business cloud and cybersecurity.
- Industry-specific connectivity and communication needs.

Do not create keyword-stuffed or near-duplicate city pages.

## 2. Metadata templates
### Home
- Title: `Business Connectivity & Communication Solutions | VMC Media`
- Description: `Explore business connectivity and communication solutions through VMC Media. Tell us your requirements and request a consultation.`
Use only after confirming brand and approved scope.

### Category
- Title: `{Category} Solutions for Businesses | VMC Media`
- Description: `Explore approved {category} options for your business. Discuss requirements, locations and next steps with VMC Media.`

### Product detail
- Title: `{Approved Product Name} for Businesses | VMC Media`
- Description: `Learn how {approved product name} may support {verified use case}. Request a consultation to discuss fit and availability.`

### Contact
- Title: `Contact VMC Media | Business Solutions Enquiry`
- Description: `Contact VMC Media to discuss approved business connectivity and communication solutions.`

## 3. On-page requirements
- Unique title and meta description per indexable route.
- One clear H1 per page.
- Descriptive H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Internal links between categories, product pages, use cases and FAQs.
- Meaningful image alt text; decorative images have empty alt.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph and social preview metadata.
- Breadcrumb navigation and eligible BreadcrumbList structured data.
- Organization/LocalBusiness structured data only for verified details and entity type.
- Product schema only when page meets applicable requirements; do not fabricate price, availability, reviews or ratings.
- Generate XML sitemap from published indexable routes.
- `robots.txt` excludes private/admin paths and staging.
- Ensure thank-you and internal search/filter URLs are not indexed unnecessarily.

## 4. Content standards
- Write original, plain-language copy.
- Explain customer problem, solution category, fit, prerequisites, limitations and next step.
- Product facts must cite current official TTBS materials internally during content review.
- Keep a record of reviewer, source URL, approval date and next review date.
- Review product copy quarterly or when provider materials change.
- Avoid guarantees and superlatives unless documented and approved.
- Never copy long passages, brochure text, images or diagrams without permission.

## 5. Content roadmap
1. Home, About, Solutions, Contact, Request a Quote and legal pages.
2. Approved solution category pages.
3. Approved product detail pages.
4. FAQs drawn from real sales questions.
5. Educational articles that answer buyer questions and point to relevant solution pages.
6. Case studies only with written customer permission and evidence.

## 6. Measurement
- Search Console: indexing, queries, CTR and page performance.
- Analytics: landing-page engagement and form conversions, subject to consent configuration.
- CRM: qualified lead and sales outcome by source.
- Avoid using vanity metrics as proof of business impact.
