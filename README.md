# Vsanco Technologies — Authorised Partner Of TATA Tele Business Services

## Project overview
A premium B2B website for **Vsanco Technologies** focused exclusively on Tata Tele Business Services (TTBS) partner solutions. Located at Regus, Logix City Centre (Near Noida City Centre Metro Station), Sector 32, Noida, Vsanco Technologies helps Indian enterprises understand relevant business connectivity and communications offerings, request consultations, and submit qualified enquiries.

**Confirmed Brand:** Vsanco Technologies — Authorised Partner Of TATA Tele Business Services.

**Important:** The site clearly indicates that Vsanco Technologies is an authorized partner of Tata Tele Business Services. It incorporates the original Tata Tele Business Services emblem, product taxonomy, and transparent partner disclaimers.

## Project principles
- Premium, trustworthy, conversion-oriented B2B design.
- Features original Tata Tele Business Services vector branding alongside Vsanco Technologies logo.
- Product taxonomy grounded in the official [TTBS Solution Finder](https://www.tatatelebusiness.com/solutionfinder/) and [TTBS Features](https://www.tatatelebusiness.com/features/).
- Scope is TTBS-partner solutions only: Internet Leased Lines, Smartflo Cloud Telephony, SIP Trunking, IVR Solutions, WhatsApp Business Platform, MPLS VPN, SD-WAN, and Cybersecurity.
- Mobile-first, accessible, SEO-ready, fast, secure, and measurable.
- All lead forms have server-side validation, consent tracking, honeypot spam protection, and durable persistence.
